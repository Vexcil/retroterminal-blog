## Ingredients


### Streusel

- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- Pinch salt
- ½ cup butter

### Filling

- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 tbsp flour
- ½ tsp cinnamon

### Cake

- 3 cups flour
- 2¼ cups sugar
- ¾ tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 cup butter
- 1 cup sour cream
- ¼ cup milk
- 4 eggs
- 1 tbsp vanilla

## Directions

1. Mix streusel; mix filling separately. Beat cake dry ingredients with softened butter until crumbly; add wet ingredients. Layer batter/filling/batter/streusel in greased 9×13-inch pan. Bake 350°F, 55 minutes. Cool 1 hour.
