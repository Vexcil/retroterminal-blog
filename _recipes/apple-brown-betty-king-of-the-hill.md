## Ingredients

- 1/2 lb Brown Sugar
- 1 tsp Ground Cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp Ground Ginger
- 1/4 tsp Ground Allspice
- 1/8 tsp Ground Cloves
- 10 Granny Smith Apples, cored and sliced
- Breadcrumbs
- 1 stick of Butter

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 375°F and butter a casserole dish. Mix the brown sugar with the spices. Core and slice the apples.

2. Layer half the apples with some spiced sugar and breadcrumbs. Repeat with the remaining apples, sugar, and breadcrumbs. Cover the top with thin slices of butter.

3. Bake covered for 20 minutes, then uncovered for 15 minutes. Cool at least 5 minutes.
