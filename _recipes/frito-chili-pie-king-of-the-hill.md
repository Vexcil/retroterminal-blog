## Ingredients

- 2 can Chili with Beans
- 1 bag of Fritos
- Cheese
- Butter, for greasing

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 375°F and grease a pie dish. Bring the canned chili to a gentle simmer.

2. Layer chili, Fritos, cheese, and more chili in the dish. Bake 20 minutes, then add another layer of cheese and Fritos and bake 5 minutes more. Rest 5 minutes.
