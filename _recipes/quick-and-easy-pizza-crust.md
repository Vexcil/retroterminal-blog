## Ingredients

- 1 cup warm water (110°F)
- ¼ oz active dry yeast
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2½ cups bread flour
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 450°F and grease a pizza pan. Dissolve yeast and sugar in water; wait 10 minutes.

2. Mix in flour, oil, and salt until smooth. Rest 5 minutes, shape a 12-inch base, and add toppings. Bake 15–20 minutes; cool 5 minutes.
