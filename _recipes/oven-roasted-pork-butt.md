## Ingredients

- 4 tsp seasoned salt
- 2 tsp dark brown sugar
- 1½ tsp sugar
- 1½ tsp paprika
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp pepper
- ⅛ tsp dry mustard
- ⅛ tsp cumin
- Pinch ground ginger
- 4–5 lb bone-in pork butt

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 350°F. Combine seasonings and rub over the trimmed, dried pork. Stand 30 minutes.

2. Roast in a foil-lined 9×13-inch pan for 4–4½ hours, to 195°F internally. Rest 15 minutes, then shred.
