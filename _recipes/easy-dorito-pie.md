## Ingredients

- 1 pound lean ground beef
- 1 (1 ounce packet) taco seasoning (about 2 tablespoons)
- ½ cup water
- 1 can refrigerated crescent rolls, crescent roll dough
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 cup shredded pepper jack cheese
- 3 cups crushed Doritos (divided)
- ¼ cup salsa
- ¼ cup melted butter

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Brown the beef, drain it, then simmer with taco seasoning and water for about 5 minutes.

2. Grease a 9-inch pie dish. Press in the crescent dough and seal its seams. Add 1½ cups crushed Doritos, then the beef.

3. Mix the salsa and sour cream and spread over the beef. Add cheese, the remaining Doritos, and melted butter. Bake about 20 minutes, until the crust is golden.
