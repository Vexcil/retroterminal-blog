## Ingredients

- 1 cup water
- 1/4 cup honey
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil or 2 tablespoons canola oil
- 3 cups white bread flour
- 1/2 cup oatmeal, uncooked
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons active dry yeast

## Directions

1. Load the ingredients in the order recommended for your bread machine.

2. Run its basic/white cycle with a medium crust and 1½-pound loaf setting, or the equivalent settings for your machine. Cool the finished loaf before slicing.
