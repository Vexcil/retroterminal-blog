## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup milk, warmed
- 6 tablespoons soft butter
- 1 egg, beaten
- 3 cups bread flour
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/2 teaspoons active dry yeast
- Melted butter, for brushing

## Directions

1. Load the bread-machine ingredients in the manufacturer&#x27;s order and run the dough cycle.

2. Shape into 24 rolls and place on a greased baking sheet. Cover with a damp cloth and rise about 20 minutes, until doubled.

3. Bake at 400°F for 15–20 minutes, then brush the tops with melted butter.
