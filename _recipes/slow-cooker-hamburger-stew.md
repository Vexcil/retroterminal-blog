## Ingredients

- 2 lbs. russet potatoes
- 1/2 lb. carrots (about 4 carrots)
- 1 yellow onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- ½ tsp dried rosemary
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- 1/4 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
- 1 lb. ground beef
- 1 15oz. can stewed tomatoes
- 2 cups beef broth
- 2 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 cup frozen peas

## Directions

1. Place the diced potatoes, carrots, onion, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and pepper in a slow cooker of at least 5 quarts.

2. Brown and drain the beef. Add it with the tomatoes and their juice, broth, Worcestershire sauce, and soy sauce; stir.

3. Cover and cook on High for 4 hours or Low for 8 hours. Lightly mash a few potatoes to thicken the stew, then stir in the frozen peas and adjust seasoning.
