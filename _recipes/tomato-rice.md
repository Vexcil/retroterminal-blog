## Ingredients

- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 yellow onion
- 2 Tbsp cooking oil
- 1.5 cups long grain white rice
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 1/4 tsp ground cumin
- 1 15oz. can petite diced tomatoes
- 1 Tbsp tomato paste
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Directions

1. Soften the diced onion and minced garlic in oil over medium heat. Add rice, chili powder, and cumin; stir and toast for a couple of minutes.

2. Add tomatoes with their juice, tomato paste, and broth. Stir, cover, and bring to a boil.

3. Reduce to a gentle simmer and cook covered for 20 minutes without stirring. Remove from the heat and rest, still covered, 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
