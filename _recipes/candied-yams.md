## Ingredients

- 29 oz canned yams or sweet potatoes, drained
- ¼ cup butter
- ½ cup brown sugar
- 1½ cups miniature marshmallows

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 400°F. Arrange drained sweet potatoes in a baking dish; dot with butter, then add sugar and marshmallows.

2. Bake 20–25 minutes, watching the topping, until heated through and golden.
