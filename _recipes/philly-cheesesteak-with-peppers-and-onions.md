## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons vegetable oil (divided (or as needed))
- 1 sweet yellow onion (halved and thinly sliced (2-inch slices))
- 1 red bell pepper (thinly sliced)
- 1 green bell pepper (thinly sliced)
- 2½ to 3 pounds petite sirloin (thinly sliced sirloin)
- 1 ½ teaspoons Lawry&#x27;s Seasoned Salt (or other all-purpose seasoning, divided)
- 1 teaspoon garlic pepper (divided)
- 8 hoagie rolls
- 6 tablespoons softened butter
- 7 ounces sliced Provolone cheese ((7 1-ounce slices) I used Tillamook Farmstyle Thick Cut)

## Directions

1. Chill the steak in the freezer for 20–30 minutes, then slice thinly across the grain and chop into small pieces.

2. Soften the onion and peppers in 2 tablespoons oil over medium-low heat. Transfer to a plate.

3. Cook the beef in two batches over medium-high heat, adding oil as needed. Divide the seasoned salt and garlic pepper between the batches; cook each about 4–5 minutes, until no pink remains.

4. Butter the split rolls and toast at 400°F for 3–5 minutes. Drain excess pan liquid, return the meat and vegetables, top with provolone, and cover until melted. Fill the rolls.
