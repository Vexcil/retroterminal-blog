## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons honey
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 1/2 tablespoons fresh lime juice
- 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon water
- 2 teaspoons vegetable oil
- two 6-ounce pieces salmon fillet

## Directions

1. Whisk honey, soy sauce, lime juice, mustard, and water together.

2. Heat the oil in a nonstick skillet over moderately high heat. Cook salmon 2–3 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through; transfer to plates.

3. Simmer the glaze in the same skillet for about 1 minute, stirring, then pour over the salmon.
