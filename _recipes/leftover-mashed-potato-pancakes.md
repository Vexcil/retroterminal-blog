## Ingredients

- 2 cups mashed potatoes (approximately)
- 1 -2 egg
- 1/4 cup flour
- salt
- pepper
- garlic
- onion (optional)
- chives (optional)
- cheese (optional)
- oil or Crisco, for frying
- sour cream (optional) or applesauce, for garnish (optional)

## Directions

1. Mix the mashed potatoes, egg, flour, seasonings, and any optional additions into a thick batter.

2. Heat a little frying oil or shortening in a skillet. Add ¼-cup portions of batter and fry on both sides until browned. Serve with sour cream or applesauce if desired.
